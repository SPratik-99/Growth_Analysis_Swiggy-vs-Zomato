## Detailed Rationale by Dimension

### 1. Search Momentum
- **Zomato**: The negative correlation between search interest and financials (Same-Quarter TV: `-0.2326`, MTU: `-0.6978`) represents a highly mature app-first customer relationship. Users bypass search engines and access the Zomato app directly.
- **Swiggy**: Spikes in Google Search interest strongly translate to business growth in the subsequent quarter (Lagged TV Correlation: `+0.4472`, MTU Correlation: `+0.4439`). This indicates search momentum is a reliable leading indicator for Swiggy.

### 2. Transaction-Value Growth
- **Zomato**: Food delivery transaction value rose from 7,318 Cr in Q1 FY24 to 9,757 Cr in Q4 FY26 (+33.3%).
- **Swiggy**: Transaction value grew from 5,959 Cr in Q1 FY24 to 9,005 Cr in Q4 FY26 (+51.1%). Swiggy has narrowed the absolute gap from 1,359 Cr in Q1 FY24 to just 752 Cr in Q4 FY26 due to consistent, faster growth velocity.

### 3. User Traction
- **Zomato**: Retains the lead in absolute customer engagement. The user base expanded by 7.9 million users over the 12-quarter period.
- **Swiggy**: Expanded its user base from 12.6 million to 18.3 million (+5.7 million users). Both companies show that user scale expansion is the direct driver of transaction volume growth (correlation $>0.96$).

### 4. Monetization Efficiency (Take Rate)
- **Zomato**: Take rate increased dynamically from 23.8% in Q1 FY24 to 32.03% in Q4 FY26. This indicates Zomato has successfully increased commissions, delivery fees, or advertising revenues without sacrificing transaction volume.
- **Swiggy**: Take rate remained highly stable and range-bound (between 24.42% and 25.83%), suggesting a more conservative commission and fee policy.

### 5. Profitability Path
- **Zomato**: Adjusted EBITDA margin started positive at 2.5% in Q1 FY24 and steadily climbed to 5.5% by Q4 FY26 (+3.0 pp).
- **Swiggy**: Swiggy broke even in Q3 FY24 (0.3% EBITDA margin) and rose to 3.3% by Q4 FY26. While Swiggy's slope is steeper (+4.0 pp improvement), Zomato's operations remain structurally more profitable.
